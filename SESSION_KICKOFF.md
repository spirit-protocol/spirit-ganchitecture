## The piece (one paragraph)

A blacked-out underground bunker in an old factory in Portugal. At the center: a monolith made from 6 VTV LED ticker panels — a clock counting down ~7 billion years to the sun's death. Visitors enter alone and offer their device (laptop preferred) to the monolith. The device gets *possessed* — audio and visual react. Each device contributes one planet's frequency. With many visitors the room becomes a symphony. Each device is unique (MAC address as hash, Art Blocks–style). Offline-first. Non-agentic redundancy first, agentic layer on top.
