### Architecture decisions locked
- **Frequency, not music** (Seth): each device emits a planet-frequency, stacks Tibetan-chant-style (440 + 480 = symphony). Pablo's band/instruments was illustrative only.
- **NFC tap > QR** (Pablo + Samer agree): small VTV screen displays numbers, NFC reader behind it. Tap takes phone/laptop to a Spirit Protocol site.
- **Per-visitor unique hash** (Samer): on link visit, server returns a unique number/hash. Drives that device's lights + sound + visible number on its own screen.
- **Decentralized audio / centralized lights** (Samer): per-device frequency plays on the device (no sync needed — frequencies harmonize naturally). Lights + master audio run from one controller (already self-sync). Do NOT try to sync per-device → central.
- **Light topology**: 18 meters of LED in 1-meter sticks = 18 nodes. Each connected visitor lights one node. 10 visitors = bright room, 2 = intimate. Pixel-level addressable per stick.
- **The deception (Pablo)**: the 0-9 digits on the LED ticker panels are a *fixed loop video*, can't be programmed. Visitors will think the obelisk is "speaking." Meanwhile the laptops they offer up show *real* numbers, driven by the agent. "You are the 9, he's the 7."
- **Visitor agency frame** (Pablo, citing ryoji ikeda + teamLab): "you are not controlling, you are being controlled." Visitors feel they're shaping the room, but the room is shaping them.
